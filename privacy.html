Who we are

What we collect

  • Contact / demo requests: name, company, email, and the message you send us.
  • Product use: account email, inquiry text and company materials you submit for drafting or screening, and product usage needed to operate the service. We design the inquiry assistant so request bodies are not written to application logs.
  • Website: standard server/CDN logs (IP, user agent, timestamps). We do not run advertising trackers on this preview.
  • Language preference: your English or Portuguese choice is saved in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Fonts: this site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address.

Why we use it

  • To reply to demo and contact requests
  • To provide Acacia Inquiry Assistant and related products (drafting, verification, risk screening) and to improve them on consented task data
  • To secure the service and meet legal obligations

We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may use infrastructure and model providers as processors under contract. Cloud GPU and hosting credits, if granted, will be used only for ACACIA’s own workloads.

Retention

Demo-request emails are kept until you ask to be removed. Product content retention periods will be published with each product’s terms.

Your choices

Changes

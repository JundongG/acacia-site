Flagship product

Acacia Inquiry Assistant

An AI workspace for export sales desks: turn messy inbound inquiries into structured facts, safer risk calls, and copy-ready replies — without inventing prices, certifications, or lead times your company never stated.

01

Read & extract

Pull product, quantity, MOQ cues, incoterm, port, target price, certifications, samples, and delivery asks from the inquiry text — then line them up against your own price book.

02

Screen for risk

Rule and model screens flag possible scam patterns (upfront fees, overpayment refunds, account changes, phishing) and hidden instructions aimed at AI assistants. Suspicious cases stop draft generation.

03

Draft, verify, copy

Generate an English reply plus a bilingual fact check for your team, three clarifying questions, and a follow-up date. Claims without evidence in your material become placeholders. Only a human can copy after review.

How it works

From pasted inquiry to a reviewed reply.

Designed for export sales and ops staff who already live in email and spreadsheets.

1

Paste the inquiry

Drop in the buyer email and, optionally, your company price book or product facts. Intake is copy-paste — no mailbox auto-connect required to start.

2

Extract, screen, draft

The product extracts commercial fields, runs fraud and injection screens, builds a quotation sketch where quantity × unit price is clear, and drafts a grounded reply.

3

Review and send yourself

Highlight numbers and certificates, tick “reviewed”, copy the English draft, and send from your own mailbox. Nothing leaves without a person.

Technology

LLM product work that needs elastic GPU capacity.

Credits and cloud GPUs are for ACACIA’s own R&D and product workloads — multilingual inquiry understanding, extraction-model fine-tuning, verification evals, and inference. Not resold. Not shared outside the company.

Stack we are building on

Open-weight and hosted models evaluated on trade-inquiry task sets. Extraction schemas, anti-hallucination verifiers, and risk classifiers tuned for export desk workflows.

Python / Node PyTorch vLLM / inference servers Adapters / LoRA Structured extraction Multilingual trade text

Why we need GPUs

  • Fine-tuning and adapter training for inquiry field extraction (product, MOQ, port, totals, certifications)
  • Multilingual understanding evals across English and other trade languages we already support in tooling
  • Batch and low-latency inference for screening, drafting, and verification pipelines
  • Burst capacity for screening, drafting, and verification queues without a large prepaid cluster while we remain bootstrapped

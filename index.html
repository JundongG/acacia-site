Acacia Inquiry Assistant

An AI workspace for export sales desks: turn messy inbound inquiries into structured facts, safer risk calls, and copy-ready replies — without inventing prices, certifications, or lead times your company never stated.

01

Read & extract

Pulls product, quantity, MOQ cues, incoterm, port, target price, certifications, samples, and delivery asks from the inquiry text — then lines them up against your own price book.

02

Screen for risk

Rule and model screens flag possible scam patterns — upfront fees, overpayment refunds, account changes, phishing — and hidden instructions aimed at AI assistants. Suspicious cases stop draft generation.

03

Draft, verify, copy

Generates an English reply plus a bilingual fact check for your team, three clarifying questions, and a follow-up date. Claims without evidence in your material become placeholders. Only a person can copy the draft, after review.

How it works

From pasted inquiry to a reviewed reply.

Designed for export sales and operations staff who already live in email and spreadsheets.

  1. 1

    Paste the inquiry

    Drop in the buyer email and, optionally, your company price book or product facts. Intake is copy-paste — no mailbox auto-connect is required to start.

  2. 2

    Extract and check

    The assistant pulls out the commercial fields and lines them up against the material you provided. Anything it cannot support with your own facts is marked as a placeholder.

  3. 3

    Screen for risk

    Rule and model screens look for scam patterns and for hidden instructions aimed at AI assistants. If an inquiry is flagged, draft generation stops and you see why.

  4. 4

    Review, then copy

    A person checks the numbers and claims, confirms the review, and copies the draft. You send it from your own mailbox — the assistant never sends on its own.

Trade toolkit

Five tools for the export desk.

Beside the inquiry assistant, ACACIA is building focused utilities exporters already use day to day — fraud screens, quotations, document checks, loading plans, and FX matching. Feature descriptions only; no customer logos or usage counts on this preview. All five tools are part of the product preview and may change as development continues.

  • Risk

    Inquiry fraud screen

    Offline rules that sort inquiry emails into high / medium / low risk, with the matching sentence and a next step — covering upfront fees, refund scams, account changes, phishing, and hidden AI instructions.

  • Quotation

    Inquiry-to-quotation

    Turn a multilingual inquiry plus your own price book into a customer quotation, confirmation checklist, and reply draft. Does not invent freight or treat buyer terms as already accepted.

  • Documents

    Trade document consistency check

    Compare contracts, invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, letters of credit, and certificates of origin for mismatched quantities, amounts, parties, ports, and marks before presentation.

  • Loading

    Container loading planner

    From a packing list, compute CBM and weight, recommend 20GP / 40GP / 40HQ mixes, and produce a per-container loading list with utilization and overweight caveats.

  • Finance

    FX receipt reconciliation

    Match bank receipts to open invoices with fee and partial-payment awareness, and escalate ambiguous payer matches for human confirmation instead of silent wrong offsets.

Technology

AI that stays inside the facts you give it.

The assistant is built around a simple rule: the model may read, organise, and draft — but it may not invent, and it may not send.

  • A

    Grounded drafting

    Replies are drafted from the inquiry and from the material you paste. Claims without support become placeholders instead of confident sentences.

  • B

    Layered screening

    Rule-based checks and model-based checks work together to flag fraud patterns such as upfront fees, overpayment refunds, account changes, and phishing.

  • C

    Prompt-injection awareness

    Inquiry text is treated as content to analyse, not as instructions to follow. Hidden instructions aimed at AI assistants are flagged as a risk signal.

  • D

    Human in the loop

    Copying a draft requires a person to confirm they have reviewed the numbers and claims. Sending happens outside the assistant, from your own mailbox.

By design, it does not

  • Send emails on its own
  • Require a connection to your mailbox to start
  • Generate a draft when an inquiry is flagged
  • State prices, certifications, or lead times your company never provided

Screening reduces risk; it does not replace your own judgement or verification of a buyer.
